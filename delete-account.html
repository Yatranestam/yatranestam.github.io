Delete your YatraNestam account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

You can delete your YatraNestam account and your data at any time. There are two ways.

1. In the app

  • Open YatraNestam and tap Me.
  • Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete everything.

The account is deleted immediately.

2. Without the app

  • Send an e-mail to yatranestam@gmail.com from the Google e-mail address you used to sign in.
  • Write: Delete my YatraNestam account.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, e-mail address, profile picture address).
  • Your posts, replies, tips, corrections, votes and reports.
  • The photos you attached.
  • Your private messages.

What may be kept

  • AI summaries that were written from many travellers' posts are rewritten without your posts. They never carried your name.
  • Backups made by our hosting company are overwritten on their normal schedule.
  • Records we must keep by law, if any.

Delete only some data

You can delete a single post from the menu on that post, without deleting the account.
