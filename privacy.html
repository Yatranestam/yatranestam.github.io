Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy tells you what the YatraNestam app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is run by YatraNestam. Contact: yatranestam@gmail.com

1. What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, e-mail address, Google profile picture address and an account number. In the app other people see only your first name.
  • What you write: posts, replies, tips, corrections, spots you add, ways to reach you write, your votes and your reports.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose to attach to a post.
  • Location: only when you press "Add current location" on a post. That position is saved with the post and shown to everybody. The app does not follow your location in the background. If you have allowed location, the search uses your last known position on the phone to show nearby places first; it is used for that search and is not saved.
  • Private messages: stored so that the two people in the conversation can read them. We read them only when one of the two reports the conversation.
  • Voice: when you speak a review or use the translator, the speech service of your phone turns your voice into text. YatraNestam does not record or store your voice.
  • Translator (Bhasha Nestam): the words you or the other person say or type are sent to our AI services to be translated. They are sent without your name or account and are kept only as a sentence (so that the same sentence is not translated twice), never linked to you. The phone says the translation aloud with its own voice.
  • Searches: the words typed in the search box are saved without your name so that we know which places to add.
  • Limits against misuse: to limit automatic misuse we keep a daily counter per account or per network address (IP) for one week.
  • Language, settings, the sentences you save in the translator and the places you opened lately ("Recently viewed", the last 3 days): kept only on your phone. They are not sent to us. "Clear" removes the list.
  • Advertisements: the app shows ads by Google AdMob. To show and count ads, Google collects your phone's advertising ID, your approximate location from the network address, and how you interact with the ads. In Europe and some other places Google asks your consent first; you can change it in My account, Ad privacy settings. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the phone settings (Settings, Google, Ads), after which you see ads that are not personalised.

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call history, files or payment details.

2. Why we use it

  • To show your posts to other travellers and theirs to you.
  • To rewrite a spoken review simply, to translate, and to write summaries.
  • To keep the app safe: hiding phone numbers, limits against flooding, checking reports.
  • To improve the list of places.
  • To show ads, which pay for the app (there is no subscription).

3. Who else handles the data

We do not sell your data. Your posts, name and e-mail are never given to advertisers. These companies process data for us so that the app can work:

  • Supabase: stores the database, the photos and the sign-in.
  • Google: Google sign-in, and the Gemini AI service that receives the text of posts, searches and the translator to rewrite, translate, summarise and to prepare details of a place. We use Google's free Gemini service: under Google's terms for it, Google may use the text it receives to improve its own products, and people at Google may read it for that.
  • Groq: an AI service that receives the same kind of text (posts, place details, searches) only when Gemini is busy, to do the same work.
  • Cloudflare (Workers AI): translates the app's own place details (descriptions, routes, tips), written in English, into Indian languages.
  • Your name and e-mail are never sent to these AI services. Do not write private details (phone numbers, addresses, health) in posts, tips or searches.
  • openrouteservice (HeiGIT) and OpenStreetMap (Overpass): our server sends them only the map positions of a place and of a starting point, to measure the road and to find bus routes. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Wikidata: our server asks it for the well-known places around a place (only that place's map position).
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia: place photos are loaded directly from their servers, so they see your network address like any website does.
  • Google Maps: only when you press a map button.
  • Google AdMob: shows the ads (see "Advertisements" in section 1). Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We may give information to the authorities when the law requires it.

4. What is public

Your first name, posts, replies, tips, corrections, photos and any location you add are visible to everybody who uses the app. Do not write private details in them.

5. How long we keep it

  • Until you delete the post or your account.
  • Reports and the daily misuse counters are kept as long as needed for safety.
  • Backups made by our hosting company are overwritten on their normal schedule.

6. Your choices

  • Delete a post: the menu on your post.
  • Delete your account and everything you wrote: My account, Delete my account. Or send a request from your sign-in e-mail to yatranestam@gmail.com; we delete within 30 days.
  • Switch off private messages: My account.
  • Location and microphone: you can refuse or withdraw the permission in the phone settings. Posting still works by typing.
  • To see or correct the data we hold about you, write to yatranestam@gmail.com.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database allows each person to change only his own rows. No system is perfectly safe; please do not post anything that would harm you if it became public.

8. Children

The app is not meant for people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has posted, write to us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

We may change this policy. The date at the top shows the latest change.
