Know before you go. Don't get cheated.

Real experiences of travellers in India, in your own language.

What the app does

  • Read what happened to other travellers at a place: fair prices, overcharging, good and bad food, stay and transport.
  • Speak or type your own experience in your language; it is shown to every traveller in theirs.
  • How to reach: which bus from the big railway stations and towns around a place, where to get down, how far it is.
  • Helpline numbers for every place.
