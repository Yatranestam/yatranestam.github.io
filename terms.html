Terms of use

Last updated: 11 October 2026

YatraNestam is an app where travellers tell what really happened on their trips in India, so that the next traveller saves money and is not cheated. By using the app you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who can use the app

  • Reading is free and needs no account.
  • To post, reply, add a tip, correct a detail or send a private message you sign in with your Google account and accept these terms.
  • You must be 18 years or older to post.

2. What you may post

  • Write only what really happened to you, as facts: what was asked, what you paid, what you found.
  • Amounts, distances and the names of public places are welcome. They help the next traveller most.
  • Photos must be your own.

3. What is not allowed

  • Abuse, threats, name-calling or hate against any person, caste, religion, region, language, gender or community.
  • False reviews, reviews written for payment, advertisements, or reviews about your own business or a competitor.
  • Another person's phone number, home address, photo or any private detail without their permission. Phone numbers are hidden automatically in public posts.
  • Sexual content, violence, anything illegal, or anything that harms children.
  • Calling a named person or business a criminal. Tell what happened; let the reader decide.
  • Copying text or photos that belong to someone else.

4. How we look after the content

  • Every post, reply, tip, correction, spot, summary and private message has a Report button. You can also block a traveller.
  • Content that several travellers report is hidden automatically. We check reports and remove content that breaks these terms. We may close an account that breaks them.
  • We aim to acknowledge a complaint within 24 hours and to settle it within 15 days.

5. Content written by AI

  • Your spoken or typed words are rewritten simply by an AI service before you post. You see the result and can change it. You are responsible for what you post.
  • Summaries of travellers' posts, translations, and the first details of some places are written by AI. They are marked in the app. AI can make mistakes. Please report a mistake when you see one.
  • Details marked "From public sources" or "Prepared by AI" are general information, not a traveller's experience.

6. Your content

  • Your posts stay yours. You allow YatraNestam to store them, show them to other users, translate them and summarise them inside the app.
  • You can delete a post at any time. You can delete your account and everything you wrote from My account in the app.

7. Private messages and locations

  • Share only your own number or the number of that shop or business in a private message.
  • A location that you add to a post is shown to everybody. Add it only at the place you are writing about. Never add your home.

8. No guarantee

  • Prices, routes, timings and conditions change. Information in the app comes from travellers, public sources and AI, and may be wrong or old. Check important things yourself before you spend money or travel.
  • Helpline numbers are given for convenience. In an emergency call 112.
  • YatraNestam does not sell tickets, rooms or tours and is not connected with any business named by a traveller.
  • The app is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, YatraNestam is not responsible for a loss that follows from relying on information in the app.

9. Changes and the law

  • We may change these terms. The date at the top shows the latest change. If a change is important we will show it in the app.
  • These terms follow the laws of India.

10. Contact

Complaints, questions and requests: yatranestam@gmail.com

11. Where our place information comes from

  • Villages, mandals and districts: Local Government Directory, Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Government of India (Government Open Data License - India).
  • PIN codes and post office positions: Department of Posts, Government of India (Government Open Data License - India).
  • Railway stations and the number of trains that stop at them: datameet (open data, CC0).
  • Towns and their map positions: GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).
  • Place photos: Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons.

These lists may be a few years old. Distances to stations are straight-line distances; the road is longer.
